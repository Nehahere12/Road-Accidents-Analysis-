ROAD ACCIDENT ANALYSIS DASHBOARD | EXCEL ANALYTICS <br>
<br>
PROJECT OVERVIEW <br>
This project delivers an interactive Road Accident Analysis Dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel using public road safety data. It presents key metrics around total casualties (9,625), injury severity levels, vehicle involvement types, road surface conditions, and environmental factors to support data-driven road safety strategies. <br>
<br>
KEY FEATURES AND VISUALIZATIONS <br>
- Executive KPI Scorecards: Displays overall casualties (9,625) along with severity splits including Fatal (74 / 1.84%), Serious (1,040 / 10.8%), and Slight (8,511 / 88.4%). <br>
- Monthly Trend Analysis: Line chart tracking monthly casualty shifts across 2019 to 2022, highlighting peak accident months in early winter and year-end. <br>
- Road & Surface Distribution: Visualizes casualties across Single Carriageway (7,254), Dual Carriageway (1,684), and surface states like Snow (7,741) and Wet (1,809). <br>
- Demographic & Lighting Split: Categorizes cases by Rural (5,112) vs Urban (4,512) areas and Daylight (55%) vs Darkness (45%) light conditions. <br>
- Dynamic Slicers: Interactive controls filtering data seamlessly by Weather Conditions, Wind Speeds, and Date Ranges. <br>
<br>
TECH STACK AND TOOLS USED <br>
- Microsoft Excel: Pivot Tables, Advanced Data Visualization, KPI Donut Charts, Dynamic Layout Design. <br>
- Data Slicers & Timeline Controls: Interactive cross-filtering across complex multi-year accident attributes. <br>
- Data Cleaning & Formatting: Standardizing road type, severity, and environmental categories. <br>
<br>
KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS <br>
- High Risk Road Types: Single Carriageways represent over 75% of total casualties (7,254 cases), requiring focused safety interventions. <br>
- Adverse Weather Risk: Snow and wet road surfaces are major incident drivers, accounting for the vast majority of total casualties. <br>
- Area Breakdown: Rural locations record higher casualty counts (5,112) compared to urban settings (4,512). <br>
